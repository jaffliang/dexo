What to Test

Dexo 2.0 (iOS 15+)

- linux.do / idcflare.com: sign in with username and password. Complete hCaptcha if shown. If the account has TOTP 2FA, confirm a 6-digit prompt appears (including when Discourse returns `invalid_second_factor_method`) and that a valid code signs you in.
- As a guest on linux.do / idcflare, complete the Cloudflare challenge and confirm browsing works after clearance. Sign in afterward and confirm the same clearance is reused.
- While logged in, tap an HTTPS link in a post and use Me / Settings → Open Web. Confirm the in-app browser is already signed in (e.g. cdk.linux.do, connect.linux.do OAuth). Opening linux.do/login should not hang on the iOS 15 SPA.
- Settings / Me → copy cookies: confirm Cookie-Editor JSON lands on the pasteboard for Safari import.
